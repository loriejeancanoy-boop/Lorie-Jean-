Hosting & Deployment Quick Reference

Essential definitions and steps to publish your portfolio website online.

Hosting

An online service that stores and serves your website files so other people can visit it.

Deploy / Publish

Sending your finished website files from your local workspace to a hosting service.

Repository & Commit

A repository is a project stored on GitHub. A commit is a saved set of changes.

Local vs Live

Local runs on your computer. Live is the published website with a public web address.
